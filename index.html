About LyricShards

LyricShards is a streamlined, elegant music player built ground-up for watchOS. Connect directly to your cloud storage providers, stream your catalogs, and manage custom jukebox playlists effortlessly straight from your wrist without needing your iPhone nearby.

Key Features

☁️ Cloud Integration

Connect securely with Microsoft OneDrive, Nextcloud, and Koofr to stream and play your files directly.

📀 Jukebox Playlists

Build, organize, and switch between customized playlists and classical jukebox queues seamlessly.

⌚ Crown Controls

Fine-tune volume or scroll through song selections precisely using the Digital Crown.
